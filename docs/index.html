Enough — Privacy Policy

A free Android app for fiber and metabolic health.

Last updated: July 11, 2026

The short version: Enough keeps everything on your phone. There is no account to create, no server to send your data to, and nothing is ever sold or shared. If you uninstall the app or tap “Delete my data,” it’s gone.

Who this covers

This policy explains how the Enough Android app handles your information. It applies to the app itself. It does not cover Google Play, Android, Health Connect, or any other software made by other companies.

What we collect and send off your device: nothing

Enough has no backend. It does not have user accounts, it does not connect to any server we run, and it contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. We — the people who make Enough — never receive your data, because there is nowhere for it to be sent. You can verify this: the app’s source code is public.

What is stored on your phone

To do its job, the app saves the following locally, in its own private storage on your device (a local database only Enough can read):

  • Meals you log (the food and serving size) and their timestamps
  • Weight entries you add
  • Activity entries you add
  • Your goals (weight-loss target, activity target, daily fiber target)
  • Your Prediabetes Risk Test answers and resulting score
  • App preferences (for example, whether Health Connect sync is turned on)

This information stays on your device. It is not backed up to us, synced to a cloud we control, or transmitted anywhere.

Health Connect

If you choose to connect Health Connect, Enough can read the following data types to show them alongside what you log:

  • Steps
  • Sleep
  • Weight

This is optional. The app is fully usable without it — you can turn the connection off at any time from the Settings screen, or revoke permission in Health Connect / Android settings. Data read from Health Connect is used only on your device, to display your own activity and trends. It is never sent anywhere by Enough. Enough does not share Health Connect data with any third party, and does not use it for advertising or any purpose other than showing it back to you inside the app.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information with anyone. There are no third-party partners, no advertisers, and no data brokers, because your data never leaves your phone in the first place.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You have two ways to delete everything:

  • “Delete my data” in Settings — permanently erases every meal, weight, activity entry, goal, and risk-test result stored on the device.
  • Uninstall the app — removes the app and all of its local data from your phone.

Security

Because nothing is transmitted, there is no server for anyone to breach. Your entries live in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. As with any app, keeping your device locked and up to date is the best protection for data stored on it.

Children’s privacy

Enough is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children — and it does not collect personal information from anyone off their device at all.

Not medical advice. Enough is a general wellness and self-tracking tool built on the approach used in the CDC’s diabetes prevention program. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, treat, or reverse any condition, and it does not measure or estimate A1c or any clinical value. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always talk to your doctor about your health and before making changes to your diet or activity.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. Because the app collects no contact information, we cannot notify you directly — please check back here.
